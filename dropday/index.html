Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you manage them on your iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Dropday. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com using the Apple Account you paid with.

I paid, but the app still shows the paywall

Open the paywall and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. If that does not fix it, email us.

I changed my release date. What happens to my plan?

Edit the date on the release and every task, deadline and reminder moves with it. If the new date leaves too little time for an editorial pitch, Dropday tells you.

Will my releases move to a new iPhone?

Your releases and tasks are stored only on your iPhone. They move to a new phone when you restore it from an iCloud or computer backup. There is no separate account or cloud copy.

Which iPhones get AI polish in Pitch Builder?

AI polish uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on iPhone 15 Pro and newer with it turned on in Settings. Every other iPhone still gets a full pitch from Pitch Builder's built-in templates.

How do I add the countdown widget?

The widget is free for everyone. On your Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, search for Dropday and choose Release countdown. For your Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, and add Dropday from the widget list.

How do I save or post a Promo Kit graphic?

In Promo Kit, add your cover art, pick a graphic and tap Share to Story or Share post. Choose Instagram or any app, or tap Save Image to put it in your Photos (Dropday asks once for permission to add photos). The caption is copied for you, so just paste it. Dropday Pro can turn off the Made with Dropday mark.

I'm not getting reminders

Open Settings, tap Notifications, choose Dropday and turn on Allow Notifications. Focus modes can also hold reminders back, so add Dropday to any Focus you use during the day.

Privacy policy

Effective December 2026

Dropday is built to keep your release plans on your iPhone. We do not run accounts, ads or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your releases, dates, tasks and notes.
  • Your answers in Pitch Builder and the pitches it writes. When AI polish is used, it runs on your iPhone through Apple Intelligence, not on our servers.
  • Reminders, which are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications.
  • Cover art you pick for Promo Kit. Dropday uses Apple's photo picker, so it only sees the image you choose, and keeps a copy on your iPhone to make your graphics. Graphics go into your Photos only when you tap Save Image.
  • What the widget shows: your next release and task, shared on your iPhone between the app and its widget.

We cannot see any of this, and we have no copy of it.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Purchases. Apple processes all payments. We never receive your card details, name or email.
  • Subscription status. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription. It receives a random app user ID and your purchase history from Apple, never your name, email or release data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • App Store statistics. Apple may share anonymous usage and crash data with us if you agreed to share it with app developers in your iPhone settings.

Children

Dropday is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

Deleting Dropday deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. To ask about or delete the subscription records RevenueCat holds, email us at the support address above. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
